Welcome to the **Don't Feed Machines Toolkit**. This is a curated collection of physical, digital, and cognitive tools designed to help individuals reclaim autonomy from pervasive surveillance, algorithmic manipulation, and biometric tracking.

## Toolkit Categories

We have divided the toolkit into five primary domains of defense:

- **[Acoustic Defense](/projects/acoustic-defense/laser-mic-defeater/)**
  Tools to defeat sonic surveillance, including ultrasonic jammers, voice modulators, and laser microphone blockers.
  
- **[Hardware Access](/projects/hardware-access/usb-data-blocker/)**
  Physical interventions to protect your devices from unauthorized data extraction, NFC skimming, and rogue physical connections.

- **[OpSec](/projects/opsec/data-detox-pamphlets/)**
  Operational security practices, guides, and lo-fi operational tools for maintaining privacy in the physical world.
  
- **[RF Security](/projects/rf-security/ble-tracker-sweeper/)**
  Defense against radio-frequency tracking, Bluetooth low-energy beacons, and rogue networks.

- **[Visual Evasion](/projects/visual-evasion/adversarial-clothing/)**
  Tactics for defeating computer vision, ALPR (Automated License Plate Reader) cameras, and infrared surveillance.
