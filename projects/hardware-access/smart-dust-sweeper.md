# Overview

The **Smart-Dust Sweeper** is a specialized electrostatic broom and vacuum mechanism designed to collect and neutralize micro-scale IoT sensors ("smart dust") that may have been deployed in your environment.

As corporate and state actors increasingly deploy microscopic, distributed sensory networks, traditional sweeps (like RF foxhunts or camera censuses) are becoming insufficient. The Sweeper utilizes a combination of electrostatic attraction, targeted localized EMP bursts, and physical containment to safely clear an area of smart dust.

## How it Works

1. **Electrostatic Accumulation:** The sweeping head generates a highly localized electrostatic field, causing micro-sensors (which typically rely on minimal static charges to adhere to surfaces) to detach and cling to the sweeping bristles.
2. **Containment:** The vacuum mechanism pulls the collected dust into a shielded Faraday chamber.
3. **Neutralization:** Once in the chamber, the device can optionally trigger a localized, low-yield EMP to permanently disable the logic boards and transmitters of the collected sensors.

## Usage Guide

- **Routine Sweeps:** We recommend sweeping high-risk areas (doorways, windowsills, air vents, and electronics racks) at least once a week.
- **Post-Intrusion:** If you suspect an unauthorized entry or have received a suspicious package, perform a deep sweep of the entire room.
- **Maintenance:** The Faraday containment chamber should be emptied into a secure, shielded disposal bag. Do not empty it into a standard trash bin, as some sensors may survive the neutralization process and continue transmitting from the landfill.
