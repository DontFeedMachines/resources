Facial recognition systems (like Clearview AI or local police deployments) rely on specific facial landmarks: the distance between the eyes, the bridge of the nose, and the jawline. **Adversarial Makeup (CV Dazzle)** uses asymmetrical, high-contrast makeup and hair styling to obscure these features.

This repository provides 3D-printable stencils to apply precise adversarial geometries quickly and consistently.

## Application Guide

<Steps>
1. **Base Layer:** Apply a matte foundation.
2. **Stencil Placement:** Align Stencil A with the bridge of your nose and cheekbone.
3. **High Contrast:** Use highly pigmented black or bright colored greasepaint to fill the stencil.
4. **Asymmetry:** Ensure that the left side of your face does not mirror the right side. Facial recognition models heavily weight symmetry.
</Steps>

:::note
This technique is designed to stop *automated* detection. It will make you highly visible to human observers. Use in crowded, chaotic environments (like protests) where human observers are overwhelmed.
:::
