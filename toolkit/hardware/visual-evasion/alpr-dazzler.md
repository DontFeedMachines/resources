---
title: "ALPR Dazzler"
description: "License plate frame equipped with infrared LEDs to blind Automated License Plate Readers."
---

Automated License Plate Readers (ALPRs) are mounted on police cruisers, toll booths, and street lights, logging the location of every vehicle that passes. Most ALPRs operate using near-infrared (NIR) cameras to read reflective plates day and night.

The **ALPR Dazzler** is a discrete license plate frame that emits high-intensity NIR light. To the human eye, it looks like a standard black frame. To an ALPR camera, it looks like a blinding sun flare that washes out the entire license plate.

## Power Configurations

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="12V Direct Wire (Recommended)">
    Wire the Dazzler directly to the vehicle's taillight harness. This ensures the Dazzler is active whenever the running lights are on, providing continuous protection at night when ALPR NIR flashes are most active.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Solar / Battery">
    For trailers or front plates where 12V wiring is difficult, use the lithium-ion battery pack with the solar trickle charger. Battery life is approximately 14 days without sun.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::caution[Legality]
In many jurisdictions, active countermeasures against ALPRs, or altering the visibility of a license plate, is illegal. Check your local laws before deploying.
:::
