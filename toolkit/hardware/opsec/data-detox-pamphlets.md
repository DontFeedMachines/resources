title: "Data Detox Pamphlets"

The **Data Detox Pamphlets** are a collection of single-sheet, tri-fold PDFs designed to be left in coffee shops, libraries, and community centers. They translate complex opsec concepts into accessible, grandmother-friendly steps.

## Topics Covered

:::cardgrid
  :::card{title="The Ad-Blocker Manifesto" icon="document"}
    Why uBlock Origin is the single most important piece of software you can install.
  :::
  :::card{title="Ditching WhatsApp" icon="seti:lock"}
    Migrating family group chats to Signal without causing drama.
  :::
  :::card{title="Location Services" icon="magnifier"}
    How to permanently disable location tracking on iOS and Android.
  :::
:::

:::note[Printing Instructions]
All PDFs are designed in black-and-white to save ink. Print double-sided on standard A4 or Letter paper.
:::
