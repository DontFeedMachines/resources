Welcome to the **Don't Feed Machines Toolkit**. This is a curated collection of physical, digital, and cognitive tools designed to help individuals reclaim autonomy from pervasive surveillance, algorithmic manipulation, and biometric tracking.

## Toolkit Categories

We have divided the toolkit into five primary domains of defense:


  - **[Hardware Access](Tools to physically interface with, audit, and protect devices from rogue connections.)**: /toolkit/hardware/hardware-access/smart-dust-sweeper/
  - **[Acoustic Defense](Systems to mask, jam, and spoof microphone arrays and ultrasonic sensors.)**: /toolkit/hardware/acoustic-defense/
  - **[RF Security](Defenses against wireless tracking, rogue access points, and BLE beacons.)**: /toolkit/hardware/rf-security/
  - **[Visual Evasion](Tactics and gear for defeating computer vision, facial recognition, and ALPR.)**: /toolkit/hardware/visual-evasion/
  - **[OpSec](Operational security practices, data detoxes, and physical anonymity guides.)**: /toolkit/hardware/opsec/
